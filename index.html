Privacy Policy for Digital Interest Book

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Digital Interest Book ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

By using Digital Interest Book, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: information associated with your account, such as your email address, phone number, display name, and Firebase user identifier (UID).
  • Authentication information: information needed to sign you in, including Google Sign-In information or mobile-number/OTP authentication information.
  • App data: information that you choose to enter or store in the App, such as customer records, transaction/ledger information, amounts, dates, notes, and related business records.
  • Advertising and Device Identifiers: non-personally identifiable technical data, including the Google Advertising ID (AAID/GAID), device model, operating system version, and advertising interaction metrics collected automatically by third-party advertising partners (Google AdMob) to serve, protect, and measure advertisements.
  • Technical information: limited technical information that may be provided by Firebase or Android/Google services to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the App.

2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Authenticate your identity and provide secure sign-in.
  • Provide the App's ledger, customer, and record-management features.
  • Store and synchronize your data across supported devices and sessions.
  • Serve banner and interstitial advertisements via Google AdMob within the App.
  • Send authentication messages such as OTPs when required for sign-in.
  • Protect the App against fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and security threats.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the App.
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal obligations.

3. Advertisements and Google AdMob

To support our services, the App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC).

Google AdMob may collect and process information such as your device's unique Advertising Identifier (Google Advertising ID), IP address, network carrier, and data about your interaction with advertisements to serve relevant ads, limit ad frequency, combat ad fraud, and analyze ad performance.

Users can manage, reset, or delete their Advertising ID and opt out of personalized advertisements at any time through their Android device settings: Settings → Google → Ads.

For more information on how Google collects and processes advertising data, please review Google's Privacy & Terms:
• How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
• Google Privacy Policy

4. Firebase and Google Services

The App uses services provided by Google Firebase and, where applicable, Google Sign-In. These services process information on our behalf to provide authentication, cloud database storage, application security, and related infrastructure.

Authentication and application data are securely stored using Firebase services, specifically Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.

5. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure operated by Google Firebase.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the App, namely Google Firebase (for hosting and database) and Google AdMob (for ad serving). We may also disclose information when required by law or legal process.

7. Account Deletion

How to delete your account

Users can permanently delete their Digital Interest Book account directly from the App:

  1. Open the Digital Interest Book app.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Open your Account or Profile settings.
  4. Select "Delete Account".
  5. Confirm the account deletion when prompted.

Once confirmed, the user's Firebase Authentication account and all user-associated application data (customer records, ledger transactions, notes) are permanently deleted.

If you need assistance with account deletion, please contact us at: dibook.pr@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Digital Interest Book is intended for general users and is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
